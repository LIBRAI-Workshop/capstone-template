**Theme:** AI Literacy, Collection, Preservation, or Research

## Introduction

What problem in your library or daily work does this address? How is it handled today?

## Deliverables

What did you produce? For example: a guide, a workshop, a prompt collection, a dataset, a workflow, a tool, or a prototype. Link to each one.

## Significance

Who benefits, and how? If anyone is using it, say so, and include any numbers you have.

## Skills needed

What does someone need to know to use or adapt this? Note any tools, accounts, or costs. "No coding needed" is a useful answer.

## How to reuse this

Give the steps someone at another library would follow to try this or adapt it to their own work.

## Data and rights

What data or materials did you use, and where did they come from? Note any copyright, licensing, or privacy limits. Don't include personal, patron, or restricted data in this repository.

## How it was tested

How did you check that it's accurate and useful? What did you try, and what happened? Note what it still gets wrong.

## What we learned

What worked, what didn't, and what you'd do differently.

## References

Sources, tools, and related work.

## Creators

Your name or names, with your library or role, and a link if you'd like one.
